<h3>Chris Taylor</h3>
<p>Chris is currently a Design Principal at the Home Office where he’s worked for 9 years in various roles, including Head of User-Centred Design and Head of Interaction Design.</p>

<p>He has 15+ years’ experience in user-centred design and strategic leadership for organisations in the public and private sector working both as part of in-house teams and in an agency. He is passionate about fostering design communities, establishing standards and best practices, and advocating for user-centred design at scale.</p>

<p>When not working, he likes to watch movies, run (very slowly) and make art.</p>

<h3>Gemma Barnes</h3>
<p>Gemma has been a service design practitioner for a decade after starting her career in user experience design almost 20 years ago.</p>

<p>She has worked extensively in the public and private sector, helping organisations to become service-led and user-focused. </p>

<p>Notable work includes driving forward user-centred design and service thinking within the Home Office and leading the design of a ground-breaking digital education, communication and rehabilitation service for people in prison while at the Ministry of Justice. </p>

<p>She also mentors those who are breaking into the profession. Gemma is passionate about making services visible, accessible and effective. Outside of work Gemma is a keen photographer, enjoying spending time in the great outdoors.</p>
